✦ Ancient Wisdom · Modern Science

Power Ingredients by Culture

Every ingredient MIRRR recommends has roots in its community's tradition

🥑
Orí (Shea Butter)
West African
Unrefined shea contains triterpene alcohols that suppress skin inflammation by up to 40%. Sacred to Yoruba cosmology.
🌿
Jamaican Black Castor Oil
Caribbean
Ash-roasted process raises pH to 8–9, opening cuticles for ricinoleic acid penetration. Clinical evidence for hair growth.
🌺
Argan Oil
Moroccan / Arab
71% oleic acid, vitamin E tocopherols. Sourced ethically through Berber women's cooperatives. UNESCO heritage crop.
🍚
Rice Water (Sake Kasu)
East Asian
Inositol penetrates hair shaft and repairs damage from inside. Ferulic acid UV protection. Used in Japan for 1,000+ years.
🌱
Frankincense (Boswellia)
East African
Boswellic acids inhibit 5-LOX enzyme reducing skin inflammation. Anti-aging peptide activation. Sacred in Ethiopian rituals.
🍵
Rooibos
South African
Aspalathin, unique to rooibos, blocks UV-induced oxidative stress. Indigenous Khoisan knowledge. Biodiversity hotspot crop.
🌿
Marula Oil
South African
76% oleic acid — higher than argan. Phenolic antioxidant content exceptional for melanin-rich skin. San people's skin protector.
🌸
Ubtan (Turmeric Paste)
South Asian
Curcumin inhibits melanin synthesis, reducing hyperpigmentation. Bridal skin-brightening paste used for 5,000 years.
🥥
Avocado Oil
Latina
Sterolins penetrate dermis uniquely among plant oils. Aztec "Ahuacatl" beauty oil — pre-Columbian skin medicine.
⚫
Black Seed Oil (Habbatus Sauda)
Arab
Thymoquinone: anti-inflammatory, antifungal, antioxidant. "Cure for everything except death" — Prophet Muhammad PBUH.
🪆
Castor Oil (Black)
Black American
Ricinoleic acid 90% — anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial. The LOC method's seal — locks in moisture at the cuticle.
☕
Coffee Scrub
East African
Ethiopian coffee ceremony skin ritual. Caffeine reduces cellulite appearance, boosts circulation for pre-ceremony glow.
